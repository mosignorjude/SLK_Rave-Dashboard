# SLK Rave regression checklist

This checklist follows the edge cases in Part 15 of the master prompt. “Covered” means an automated test currently exercises the behavior; “Partial” means a related test exists but does not exercise the full stated scenario; “Gap” means it still needs automated coverage or documented manual verification. Passing suites do not imply coverage of rows marked Partial or Gap.

**Current production status (2026-09-28):** The updated Hosting build and reviewed Firestore Rules were deployed to `slk-rave-db` after explicit owner approval. Firebase CLI reported success; the live site serves the new build. The older deployment observations below are historical snapshots. Live role-specific behavior, recovery, and App Check token execution still need validation; the owner deferred the Spark-plan backup process review until after production.
